## Blood markers (critical for product design)

- ✅ **Lactose gene test (LCT-13910): validated, 100% specificity, 93% sensitivity** (Gut 2004, PMID 15479673). The ONLY solid blood marker we have. Caveat: European-population-specific.
- ✅ **DAO/histamine: DGAKI guideline advises AGAINST serum DAO as standalone test** (AWMF 061-030l). No validated standalone histamine test. Must not be sold as valid.
- **Fructose, sorbitol, FODMAPs: no blood test exists at all.**
