# Open decisions

## 1. Kit composition (the DAO problem)

The "Honest Test" kit has only ONE solid blood marker: the lactose gene test. DAO/histamine is actively advised against by the DGAKI guideline - selling it as valid would break the whole "honest" positioning.

Options:
- a) Blood kit with lactose only + app for everything else (honest, but is one marker enough to justify a fingerprick kit?)
- b) Physical kit WITHOUT blood: standardized challenge portions for test days + symptom cards + app (shelf presence without lab logistics)
- c) Pure app, 79 EUR (cleanest, but no tangible product)

**Status: open.** Stage 1 could test (a) vs (c) messaging on the landing page.

## 2. Lead product: kit-first or scanner-first?

Scanner ("Yuka for intolerances") could be the acquisition engine instead of a later layer. Kit-first = revenue-first; scanner-first = growth-first.

**Status: open, kit-first is the current assumption.**
