# Concept

## The problem

- **Everyone suspects, nobody knows.** 1 in 3 Austrians report a food intolerance, but only ~2 in 10 of the symptomatic ever get a diagnosis. Most restrict the wrong foods for years.
- **The honest path is broken.** H2 breath tests aren't covered by ÖGK (up to 240 EUR, one appointment per sugar, ~60-day waits). For histamine no valid lab test exists at all.
- **The shelf is full of fake answers.** IgG kits (70-190 EUR) sell in every pharmacy even though every allergy society rejects them. KONSUMENT rated 0 of 10 kits recommendable.

## The product

**Hybrid kit: test + guided self-experiment.**

1. **A valid test where one exists** - finger-prick lactose gene test (LCT-13910, the only validated blood marker). Never IgG.
2. **The gold-standard method for the rest** - guided elimination + reintroduction with symptom tracking for fructose, sorbitol, histamine and FODMAPs. Ends in a personal Verträglichkeitsprofil.
3. **An answer that works in daily life** - Austrian product swaps (Spar, Billa, dm), recipes, eating-out help. From home, for the price of one fake kit.

## Program flow (4-6 weeks)

1. Week 1: baseline with AI photo logging, no restrictions
2. Targeted elimination of top suspects only (not blanket elimination)
3. Structured test days (reintroduction, one trigger at a time)
4. Result: Verträglichkeitsprofil + Alltag layer kicks in

## Target market

Adults 25-55 in Austria (then DACH) with recurring, unexplained gut symptoms and no diagnosis - the "suspecting undiagnosed", ~1.9M people. Female-skewing, urban, health-engaged, already buying frei-von products. Not for diagnosed conditions (red-flag redirect to physician).

Reachable via: FruLak & Co, histamine Facebook group (~7k), Histaminikus (~22k IG), HistaFood (~21k IG).

## Positioning

- **Credibility moat:** "We don't sell IgG tests because they don't work" - a stance kit vendors can't copy. Earns free PR (KONSUMENT 0/10).
- **Radically independent:** no ads, no industry money, no IgG (Yuka playbook).
- **Local:** German-language, Austrian-shelf-aware. Monash is English/FODMAP-only, Fig is US-products-only, Cara Care is prescription-locked and out of Austria until ~2027.

## Regulatory guardrails

- Wellness wording only - never "Diagnose" or "Test" as medical claim (MDR Rule 11 → Class IIa risk)
- Healthy users only (MTD-Gesetz), red-flag symptoms → redirect to physician
- No IgG, ever. DAO/histamine blood marker must NOT be sold as valid (DGAKI guideline actively advises against it)

## Later layers

Product scanner, shareable Gut Pass, anonymized B2B retail insights.
