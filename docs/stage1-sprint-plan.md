# Stage 1 sprint plan (2 weeks, budget 1.2k)

Following the Luumi playbook: landing page + paid traffic test + interviews → Pecha Kucha → vote.

## Core question

**Do people buy a 30-day guided self-experiment (with one honest test) instead of an "instant" fake 99 EUR result sitting next to it on the shelf?**

## Week 1

- [ ] Landing page (DE): hero, problem, how it works, pricing, pre-order/waitlist CTA
  - Test two framings if possible: "Honest kit" (99-129 EUR) vs "Program" (79 EUR)
- [ ] Set up analytics (conversion on pre-order intent, not just email)
- [ ] Google/Meta ads setup - keywords around "Lebensmittelunverträglichkeit Test", "Laktoseintoleranz Test", "Histaminintoleranz Symptome" (people searching for the IgG kits ARE the audience)
- [ ] Interview guide + recruiting start in communities (FruLak & Co, HIT Facebook group ~7k, Histaminikus, HistaFood)

## Week 2

- [ ] 8-10 interviews with "suspecting undiagnosed" adults
  - What have they already tried? Did they buy an IgG kit? Why (not)?
  - Would they wait 4-6 weeks for a real answer vs instant fake result?
  - Price sensitivity: 79 vs 99-129 EUR
- [ ] Ad test running, monitor CTR + landing conversion
- [ ] Pecha Kucha deck
- [ ] Decision memo: kit vs pure app, pricing, go/no-go for Stage 2

## Success signals (define upfront)

- Landing conversion to pre-order intent: target >3-5% from paid traffic
- Interviews: majority confirms "guesswork pain" > "want instant result"
- CPC on intolerance keywords low enough for viable CAC at 99-129 EUR AOV

## Assets needed

- Landing page (reuse 120V boilerplate / Netlify like Luumi)
- Ad creatives (can use meta-ads skill)
- Interview guide
